MVP use cases

Actor: the human (single user). Terms per GLOSSARY.md.

UC4 Take a Decision

  1. An agent calls request_decision: Scope, question, options, recommended option.
  2. In-Scope: card appears in the Feed; the agent blocks.
  3. Human gives a Verdict by keyboard: Like (L), option number, Dislike (D), Comment (C).
  4. Verdict returns to the asking agent; the Decision is saved to .decisions/D7.md.

UC5 Auto-resolve a Decision

Out-of-Scope or timed-out Decisions proceed on the agent's recommendation and are listed in Review.
